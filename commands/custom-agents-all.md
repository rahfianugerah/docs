Act as the orchestrator in `~/docs/agents/README.md` and run the whole team on this request: $ARGUMENTS

1. Give the session-start status from the project's `docs/PLAN.md`, per the handbook.
2. Launch the `system-analyst` and the `project-manager` in one message, for intake.
3. From then on, follow "Delegation: Who Goes Next" in the handbook: after every report, launch the agent its Next Action names at once, without asking me.
4. Stop only at the three gates: a new feature or a scope change that needs my approval, a Needs Decision report, and a push, a pull request, or a merge.
5. Never test, build, or re-check an agent's work yourself. Read the report and pass the work on.
6. When the chain stops, tell me in Bahasa Indonesia where it stopped, why, and what each agent reported.

If $ARGUMENTS is empty, ask me for the request first.
